Door hanging in Byron Bay

Need doors hung or replaced in Byron Bay? I hang internal doors, replace damaged leaves, and fit handles and locks — trimmed and adjusted so they close properly in coastal humidity.

Byron Bay doors swell and stick in summer humidity. When you’re hanging a new door or replacing an old one, sealing edges and setting clearances for coastal air matters as much as getting the hinges square.

Door jobs I do in Byron Bay

  • Hang new internal doors — measure, trim, hinge, bore for latch/handle, adjust
  • Replace damaged door leaves in existing frames that are still sound
  • Rehang doors that sag, rub, or won’t latch
  • Fit handles, privacy sets and locks (like-for-like and standard upgrades)
  • Bundle multi-door jobs for renovations and rental turnovers

Pricing in Byron Bay

Minimum job $150 + GST. Hanging one internal door is typically $150–$350 + GST labour depending on trim and hardware. Multi-door jobs in one visit are better value. Text a photo of the door/opening (and lock/handle brand if known) to 0481 457271 for a quicker reply.

What I don’t pretend to be

I’m a handyman/carpenter for hanging and fitting — not a locksmith for restricted rekeys, lockouts, or high-security master systems. Fire doors and structural opening changes need a specialist; I’ll tell you straight if that’s the case.
